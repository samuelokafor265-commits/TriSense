# TriSense
It's a general-purpose portable sensing node, so it fits anywhere you need to measure pressure and distance wirelessly on battery power:
Weather and environment monitoring: the pressure sensor tracks barometric changes and altitude, and the ESP32's Wi-Fi/Bluetooth sends data to a phone or cloud dashboard.
Drones and robots: altitude hold from the pressure sensor, plus obstacle avoidance or landing-height sensing from the time-of-flight sensor.
Proximity and occupancy detection: smart doors, people counting, or gesture and presence sensing.
Liquid or fill-level monitoring: the ToF sensor measures distance to the surface in tanks, bins, or water reservoirs.
Indoor navigation or floor detection: pressure changes can tell which floor of a building a device is on.
Data loggers and IoT nodes: with the LiPo and USB charging, it can run untethered in the field, which suits remote or off-grid deployments such as solar-powered stations.
Teaching and prototyping: it's a good reference board for learning I2C, level shifting, and power-path design.
The light-sensing circuit (LDR with a transistor driving an LED) could add automatic lighting or day/night detection.
